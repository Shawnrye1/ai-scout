# DESIGN TIPS

1. **Keep text minimal** - These slides have more text than ideal for presenting. When you put them in Slides, use larger fonts and fewer words.

2. **Use visuals** - Add screenshots from your app on slides 4, 6, and anywhere else you can.

3. **Consistent colors** - Stick to your brand blue (#0f2d52), white, and one accent color.

4. **One idea per slide** - If a slide feels crowded, split it.

5. **Big numbers** - Make metrics (500+, 90%+, etc.) very large and bold.

6. **Practice** - This deck should take 5-7 minutes to present. Each slide = 30 seconds.
