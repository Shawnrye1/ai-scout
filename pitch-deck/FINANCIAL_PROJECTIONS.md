## Key Assumptions

### Growth Assumptions
- 10% monthly churn (net of reactivations, seasonal)
- 2:1 expansion revenue on upgrades
- 85% of growth from organic/referral (Year 1)
- Adding 1 new sport per quarter starting Q2 Y1

### Cost Assumptions
- **GPU cost per game:** $3 average
- **Games per customer per month:** 8 average
- **GPU cost per customer/month:** $24
- **Gross margin:** 79% ($115 ARPU - $24 GPU = $91)

## Scenario Analysis

### Bull Case (2x growth)
- Faster coach adoption via viral growth
- Athletic department deals accelerate
- Year 3 ARR: $10M+

### Base Case
- Steady organic growth
- Normal sales cycle
- Year 3 ARR: $5M

### Bear Case (0.5x growth)
- Slower adoption than expected
- Longer sales cycles
- Year 3 ARR: $2.5M
- Still achieves profitability

---

## Key Risks to Model

1. **Churn higher than expected** - Sports are seasonal; some coaches may cancel in off-season
2. **GPU costs increase** - Dependent on Modal.com pricing; could build own infra at scale
3. **Slower sales cycles** - Athletic department sales may take 6+ months
4. **Competition** - If Hudl or others enter, may need more marketing spend
