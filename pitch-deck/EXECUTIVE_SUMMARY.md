# AI Scout - Executive Summary

## One-Liner
AI Scout automatically generates professional scouting reports from game film, saving coaches 90%+ of their film review time.

---

## The Problem
High school and college coaches spend 6+ hours manually reviewing game film after every game. This leaves no time for actual coaching, leads to subjective evaluations, and causes massive burnout. Meanwhile, talented athletes go unnoticed because scouts can't watch every game.

## The Solution
Upload game film (or paste a Hudl/YouTube link) and receive detailed, AI-generated scouting reports for every player in under an hour. Our computer vision pipeline automatically detects players, reads jersey numbers, and analyzes performance—no manual tagging required.
