# AI Scout - Target Investors

## Ideal Investor Profile

### Pre-Seed Characteristics
- Check size: $25K - $100K
- Comfortable with solo founders
- Understands SaaS / AI / sports tech
- Can add value beyond capital (intros, advice)
- Doesn't require board seat at this stage

### Value-Add Areas We Need
1. Sports industry connections (coaches, ADs, leagues)
2. AI/ML technical expertise
3. B2B SaaS scaling experience
4. Recruiting/hiring help

## Outreach Strategy

### Warm Intro Sources
1. **LinkedIn connections** - Who do you know that knows them?
2. **Founder networks** - Other founders who've raised
3. **Accelerator alumni** - If you've done any programs
4. **Coach network** - Coaches may know investors

### Cold Outreach Channels
1. **Email** - Best for VCs (find via Crunchbase, firm websites)
2. **LinkedIn** - Good for angels
3. **Twitter DM** - Some investors prefer this

### Timing
- **Best months:** Jan-Mar, Sep-Nov
- **Avoid:** Aug (vacation), Dec (holidays)
- **VC partner meetings:** Usually Mon-Thu
- **Follow up:** 3-5 business days

## Pre-Seed Round Structure

### Recommended: SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity)

**Standard Terms:**
- Post-money SAFE (YC standard)
- $3-4M valuation cap
- No discount (or 20% if needed)
- No pro-rata rights at this stage

**Alternative: Priced Round**
- Only if lead investor requires
- More legal cost ($10-20K)
- Sets formal valuation

### Round Composition Target
- 1-2 lead investors ($100-150K each)
- 5-10 smaller angels ($25-50K each)
- Total: $400-600K

## Diligence Preparation

### Documents to Have Ready
1. Pitch deck (PDF, 15 slides max)
2. Executive summary (1-page)
3. Financial model (Google Sheets)
4. Product demo (live or video)
5. Customer references (2-3 coaches)

### Common Diligence Questions
- Technical architecture deep-dive
- Customer acquisition strategy
- Competitive landscape
- Founder background/motivation
- Use of funds breakdown

### Reference Checks They'll Do
- Talk to your customers
- LinkedIn mutual connections
- GitHub/portfolio review
- Background check (at signing)

## Tracking & CRM

### Recommended Tools
- **Notion** - Pipeline tracking
- **Affinity** - Relationship intelligence
- **Google Sheets** - Simple and free

### Pipeline Stages
1. **Identified** - On target list
2. **Reached Out** - Email/DM sent
3. **Responded** - Got a reply
4. **Meeting Scheduled** - Call booked
5. **Met** - Had first meeting
6. **Follow-up** - Sent materials, awaiting decision
7. **Committed** - Verbal yes
8. **Signed** - SAFE signed
9. **Funded** - Money in bank
10. **Passed** - Said no

### Metrics to Track
- Response rate (aim for 10-20%)
- Meeting conversion (aim for 50%+)
- Commitment rate (aim for 10-20% of meetings)
- Time to close (typically 2-6 weeks)

## Closing the Round

### When to Stop Raising
- Hit your target ($500K)
- Have 6+ months runway
- Key milestones funded

### Post-Commitment Steps
1. Send SAFE (use YC template)
2. Collect signatures (DocuSign)
3. Provide wiring instructions
4. Confirm receipt
5. Send thank you + first update

### After Funding
- Monthly investor updates
- Ask for intros when relevant
- Keep them engaged (they're your advocates)

---

*Focus on quality over quantity. 20 warm intros > 100 cold emails.*
