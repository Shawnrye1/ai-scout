## Data Room Checklist

For investors doing due diligence, prepare:

1. **Pitch deck** (PDF)
2. **Executive summary** (1-pager)
3. **Financial projections** (spreadsheet)
4. **Cap table** (current ownership)
5. **Product demo** (video or live link)
6. **Customer references** (2-3 coaches willing to chat)
7. **Technical architecture** (1-page overview)
8. **Team bios** (LinkedIn links)
9. **Incorporation docs** (if formed)
10. **Terms** (SAFE or term sheet template)
